Key Findings
The build is technically clean: 0 pages are missing a title, meta description, canonical, or viewport, and all 53 carry rich JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, Product + a 4.9/300 AggregateRating, plus FAQPage on keyword pages and Article on blog posts). However, the prerenderer leaves the template's static SEO tags in place and injects new ones, so every page ships with two <meta name="description"> and two og:title tags — and the generic template copy comes first, which is what Google reads. Separately, 26 of 53 titles exceed 60 characters and all 53 meta descriptions exceed 160, so both truncate in results. The homepage exists at both / and /nl with self-referencing canonicals (duplicate content), and its title doubles the brand: "NEDERLAND IPTV — NEDERLAND IPTV". On architecture, 14 landing pages are orphans (no internal links point to them), so they get almost no crawl priority or link equity. Finally, content is thin for a competitive niche — blog posts average 375 words and keyword pages 136–411, while the Dutch IPTV SERP is dominated by 1,500–3,000-word comparison articles.
⚠ Note on the earlier keyword-page cleanup
Your own keyword data shows the competitor-brand pages we removed carry real volume: pandora iptv (3,600/mo), iptv dark (1,300), hakuna iptv (720), iptv hot (720), nero iptv (720), plus never-built diamond iptv (1,000) and omni iptv (720) — roughly 8,800 searches/month of (mostly navigational/commercial) demand now uncaptured. Competitors rank by publishing "[Brand] IPTV review / alternative" pages that redirect that intent. This was your call to keep the template clean, but it's a measurable traffic trade-off — flagged here so you can decide per brand.

Structured Data Risk

Product + 4.9 AggregateRating injected on all 53 pages — including 30 blog articles

Great for review stars on commercial pages, but Google's review-snippet guidelines want the rating to describe the page's primary subject. A product rating stamped onto informational blog posts can be ignored or, at worst, trigger a structured-data manual action.

Keep Product+AggregateRating on the homepage and keyword/landing pages; on blog posts emit only Article/BlogPosting schema (drop the Product graph there).
Medium
Security & Server Headers

Not verifiable from a static build

HTTPS, HSTS, CSP, compression (gzip/brotli) and cache headers depend on the host (Vercel/Netlify/nginx). This audit can't measure them locally.

After deploy, confirm HTTPS + HSTS, enable brotli, and set long cache lifetimes on /assets/* (Vite already fingerprints them).
